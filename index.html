About Me

When I was 14, I decided that I wanted to become an engineer. I was drawn to the idea of building things, solving problems and understanding how technology works. That goal stayed with me as I finished school and moved toward engineering.

In 2022, I joined Modern Academy for Engineering and Technology as a Computer Engineering student. University introduced me to programming, databases, networks and software engineering. With each course and project, I started to understand that software was the part of engineering I wanted to keep exploring.

My focus gradually moved toward backend and full-stack development. I began working with C#, .NET, SQL and web technologies, then added practical training through DEPI, Route Academy, Cisco and Flutter training. Each experience helped me connect what I learned in class with real development work.

Today, I am building my path as a Computer Engineering student with a focus on Full Stack .NET development. I learn by building, improving projects and understanding why each part of the system works. My goal is to graduate in 2027 with a strong foundation and a portfolio that shows how far that journey has taken me.
